The current estimate was reviewed against the prior forecast after several activities moved into a later period. The main difference appears in the monthly profile rather than in the underlying direction of the plan. The figures were therefore assessed together with expected activity, existing commitments and items that are still subject to confirmation.

The review reconciled the management summary with the supporting records and separated amounts affecting the current period from those expected to reverse in a subsequent cycle. Several movements require additional context before they can be interpreted as changes in underlying performance. An earlier version of the forecast was retained to distinguish the effect of the revised timetable from normal period-to-period variation.

The transition is expected to increase expenditure in the short term because additional work will be required to stabilise the process after implementation. The longer-term cost profile remains broadly consistent with the previous view, although the timing of the impact has changed. The committee requested a clearer distinction between recurring operating effects and costs that arise only during the transition.

Some amounts could not yet be fully confirmed because the operational records and the financial summary were prepared at different points in the reporting cycle. The outstanding items are being checked with the responsible teams and will be incorporated once the supporting information is available. No material change to the overall baseline has been identified at this stage.

The comparison with the previous period also showed a movement in several activities that should not be interpreted in isolation. The effect is partly explained by timing and partly by commitments already recorded. The current working assumption is that the remaining uncertainty will be resolved at the next reporting checkpoint.

The proposed course of action remains reversible while the assumptions are being validated. The supporting material includes observations from earlier cycles because they help explain how the current position was reached. The final update should present the current estimate, the main drivers of the movement and the items that remain dependent on further confirmation.
